House rules

House rules

So that everyone can relax and misunderstandings are avoided, please read these carefully. By booking and paying, guests accept the house rules.

  • Check-in15:00–22:00
  • Check-outby 10:00
  • SmokingNot allowed
  • PetsNot allowed
Check-in and check-out

The apartment is available from 15:00 on the day of arrival until 10:00 on the day of departure. Arrival is possible between 15:00 and 22:00.

Earlier check-in or later check-out is possible for an extra fee, by prior arrangement.

No refund is given for early departure.

Registration and payment

On arrival or beforehand (online or in person), guests must provide their personal documents for registration (passport or ID card, proof of address) and pay any amount due on site. The apartment can only be occupied once the full price has been paid.

We are happy to welcome guests in person on request, but contactless arrival is also possible.

Keys and codes

On arrival, guests receive the code for the stairwell door and the key to the apartment door, which is kept in the key box next to the door in the stairwell.

On departure, please return the key to the key box and lock it with the code given on arrival. If a key is lost, please report it immediately – the cost of the key and of changing the lock is payable by the guest.

Smoking

Smoking is strictly prohibited in the building and in the apartment – including on the balcony.

Parking

The apartment has no private parking. In front of the building, parking is paid during the day (Mon–Fri 8:00–18:00, Sat 8:00–14:00) and limited to a maximum of 3 hours (red zone).

In nearby streets a day ticket can be bought (depending on the street, orange zone I or green zone II) – we are happy to help on request. Buy a day ticket (Hungarian)

Guests and visitors

Only people whose ID has been shown to the host may stay in the apartment, and only up to the number of guests stated at booking. Please list every guest when booking, including children of any age.

The host may check, without prior notice, that the number of guests matches what was booked and paid for. Visitors are not allowed.

Quiet and consideration

Out of respect for the neighbours, please avoid noisy activities and move quietly in the stairwell.

Under the building’s own house rules, noisy activity from Monday to Saturday between 7:00 and 20:00, and all day on Sundays, counts as a nuisance. Breaching the building’s house rules may have legal consequences.

Pets

Pets are not allowed. Please take this rule seriously – some of our guests are allergic to animal hair.

Cleaning

The accommodation fee paid on arrival includes final cleaning. On longer stays, the apartment is cleaned again and bed linen and towels are changed on day 7.

Extra daily cleaning is available for an additional fee – please let us know in advance.

Waste

The waste bins are under the kitchen sink. Please separate your waste: bin 1 for general waste, bin 2 for paper, plastic and metal cans.

When leaving, please take the rubbish down to the bins in the stairwell (blue bin – general waste, yellow bin – recycling).

Order, meals and damage

Please keep the apartment tidy, look after the furnishings and do not take anything out of the apartment. Please eat in the kitchen rather than in the rooms, and keep it clean.

Any damage caused is to be paid for by the person responsible or their legal representative.

TV, internet and blinds

The TV subscription includes 44 channels, and Wi-Fi is provided in the apartment.

The apartment has electric roller blinds, operated with the remote control found in each room: the up arrow raises them, the down arrow lowers them.

Breach of the house rules

The owner has the right to cancel the stay if a guest does not follow the house rules. In that case, any remaining booking fee cannot be refunded.

By using the accommodation, guests confirm that they have read, understood and accepted the house rules.

If you have any other question, request or problem, please feel free to get in touch.
